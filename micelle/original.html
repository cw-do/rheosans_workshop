RheoSANS: wormlike micelle alignment

When the bob rotates in a Couette cell, CTAB wormlike micelles in the gap align along the flow direction. Depending on where the beam passes, you see the same alignment in the 1–3 or the 2–3 plane.

Schematic, not to scale (real gap is about 0.5–1 mm, micelle diameter about 4–5 nm). The 2D pattern is qualitative: a form factor computed from the segment orientations in panel ①, multiplied by a simple S(q) peak whose position and strength are arbitrary. At low Wi the alignment axis is tilted from flow toward the gradient direction, so a weak anisotropy remains in the 2–3 pattern. Shear banding is not included.
